Interconnections

Every word belongs to one row and one column.

How To Play

Sort the nine words into a 3×3 grid of connections in 3 tries.

  • Every row and every column shares a connection, and each word belongs to one row and one column.
  • Tap a tile, then another, to swap them. Press Submit to check. Revert undoes swaps you haven't submitted.
  • The rows and columns start unlabelled. The game picks the setup that best fits your grid, and the colour of each tile shows how close it is.
  • When a whole row or column is green, its connection is revealed.

Examples

ApplePlumLemon

Apple is in the correct row and the correct column. It locks in place.

SoupPearRice

Pear is in the correct row or the correct column, but not both.

ForkGoldBrick

Brick is in neither the correct row nor the correct column.

Hard mode

Press the lightning button before your first guess. The rows and columns are then fixed from the start instead of adapting to your guess, so a group in the wrong place scores nothing and you need the exact layout.

A new puzzle is released daily at midnight.
